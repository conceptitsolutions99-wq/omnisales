Understanding the OmniSales Platform

OmniSales is your complete outbound sales system targeting Canadian and USA businesses for IT solutions:

  • Dashboard: View your personal metrics, call logs, and pipeline value
  • Leads Generator: Access and filter business leads by country, size, and solution type
  • The Dialler: Make calls via tel: protocol (Phone Link, Skype, WhatsApp) with real-time logging
  • Analytics: Track your conversion rate and call outcomes
  • Follow-ups: Manage callbacks and payment transfer confirmations
💡 Pro Tip
You only see YOUR OWN data as a sales agent. Admin users see company-wide metrics.

Your First Login & Setup

When you log in for the first time, familiarize yourself with these key areas:

  • Check your user role and permissions in the top navigation bar
  • Review the Dashboard to understand your current pipeline
  • Test your calling setup (WhatsApp, Skype, or Phone Link)
  • Set your preferred theme (light/dark mode)

Making Effective Calls
▼

The Perfect Cold Call Structure

Introduction (15 seconds):

Script
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from Concept IT Solutions. We help [business type] companies in [Canada/USA] improve their digital presence. Do you have 2 minutes to chat?"

Value Proposition (30 seconds):

Script
"We recently helped [similar company] increase their online sales by 40% with a custom e-commerce platform. I noticed [their company] is in [industry] and thought you might benefit from a similar solution."

Qualifying Questions (2-3 minutes):

  • Ask about current challenges with their website/systems
  • Identify decision-makers and budget authority
  • Gauge timeline and urgency

Next Steps (30 seconds):

Script
"Based on what you've shared, I'd love to send you a brief proposal outlining how we can help. When would be a good time for a 15-minute follow-up call next week?"

Handling Common Objections

Objection: "We already have a website."

Response
"That's great! Many of our clients had websites but wanted to modernize or add functionality like online ordering, customer portals, or mobile apps. When was your site last updated?"

Objection: "It's too expensive."

Response
"I understand budget is important. Most clients see this as an investment that pays for itself. For example, if it brings in just 2-3 additional clients per month, it's already covered. We also offer flexible payment terms."

Objection: "Send me some information."

Response
"Absolutely! To make sure I send you the most relevant information, can I ask just 2 quick questions about your needs?" [Then qualify them]

Using The Dialler System
▼

Step-by-Step Dialling Process

  • Step 1: Select a lead from the Leads Generator or Dashboard
  • Step 2: Click "Call Now" - this opens your device's calling app (Phone Link/Skype/WhatsApp)
  • Step 3: The timer starts automatically when you click "Start Call"
  • Step 4: Make your call following the script structure
  • Step 5: Click "End Call" when finished
  • Step 6: Select outcome: Considering, Deal Completed, Denied, or Call Back Later
  • Step 7: Add detailed notes about the conversation
  • Step 8: Click "Save Call Log & Update Lead"
💡 Important
Always add detailed notes! Future you (or your team) will thank you when following up.

Call Outcome Definitions

✅ Deal Completed: Client verbally agreed and is ready to sign/pay

🤔 Considering: Interested, asked for proposal, needs time to think

❌ Denied: Not interested, not a fit, or explicitly rejected

📞 Call Back Later: Asked to call back at specific time, or couldn't reach decision-maker

Closing Deals & Follow-ups
▼

Closing Techniques

The Assumptive Close:

Example
"Great! I'll send over the agreement today. Would you prefer to start with the website or the mobile app first?"

The Summary Close:

Example
"So we've discussed a custom e-commerce site at $18K with mobile-responsive design, SEO optimization, and ongoing support. Sound good?"

The Urgency Close:

Example
"We have availability in our development queue this month. If we start now, we can have you live by [date]. Shall I reserve that slot?"

Managing Follow-ups

  • Create follow-up task immediately after "Considering" or "Call Back" outcomes
  • Schedule specific date and time (not "next week")
  • Add context notes: what to discuss, what was promised
  • Check Follow-ups tab daily at start of shift
  • Update status after completing follow-up call
🎯 Success Metric
Top performers follow up within 24-48 hours. Speed matters in sales!
